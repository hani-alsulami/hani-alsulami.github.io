Saudi cybersecurity regulation

A starting point for security teams reviewing national cybersecurity controls and financial-sector requirements in Saudi Arabia.

NCA controls ↓SAMA framework ↓

National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)

الهيئة الوطنية للأمن السيبراني

NCA publishes national cybersecurity controls and implementation guidance. Start with the scope in each control document.

Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC 2:2024)

Baseline controls for entities within the published scope, covering governance, defense, resilience and third-party cybersecurity.

Cloud Cybersecurity Controls (CCC 2:2024)

Cloud-specific controls extending ECC. Review the scope and requirements for the provider or tenant role that applies.

NCA implementation guides

Practical guidance for applying the published controls.

Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)

البنك المركزي السعودي

SAMA publishes cybersecurity requirements for regulated financial institutions. Use the Rulebook to identify requirements relevant to your institution.

SAMA Cyber Security Framework

Domains include leadership and governance, risk management and compliance, operations and technology, and third-party cybersecurity.

Framework applicability

Official guidance on entities and requirements within the framework’s scope.

Start with applicability

Check the entity type and services in scope, then record the official version and control references. Assign an owner and document the evidence that demonstrates each applicable control. Where multiple frameworks apply, keep each requirement traceable to its official source.
