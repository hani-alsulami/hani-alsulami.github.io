Applying the controls

Use the workflows, evidence and measures as implementation examples. Confirm applicability and reporting obligations against the official texts.

CTI · Intelligence that leads to action

NCA ECC 2-13-3.4 and 2-13-3.5 · Sharing and intelligence feeds SAMA CSF 3.3.16 · Threat management SAMA financial-sector CTI principles
Workflow
Define intelligence requirements around critical services and relevant threats. Record source credibility, relevance and confidence. Send actionable findings to the right owner with handling restrictions, an expiration date where appropriate, and a way to report the outcome.
Evidence examples
Intelligence requirements, source register, an assessment with its confidence rationale, dissemination record, and a linked SOC investigation or detection coverage ticket.
Operational measures
Time from a relevant advisory to a documented triage decision, and the share of high-priority intelligence items that led to a recorded action.

SOC · Monitoring and triage

NCA ECC 2-12 · Event logs and monitoring SAMA CSF 3.3.14 · Event management
Workflow
Inventory critical log sources and their owners. Monitor ingestion from critical systems, privileged accounts and remote access. Assign alert severity, record the analyst’s decision, and open an incident when escalation criteria are met.
Evidence examples
Approved log-source inventory, collection and retention settings, a sample log retrieval, ingestion failure tickets, and an alert with the analyst’s triage notes and escalation timestamps.
Operational measures
Share of expected log sources reporting within their agreed window, median time from alert creation to triage, and the number of unresolved telemetry gaps by severity.

IR · Response and recovery

NCA ECC 2-13-3.1 to 2-13-3.4 · Response, classification and reporting SAMA CSF 3.3.15 · Incident management
Workflow
Classify the incident and name the response lead. Preserve evidence, record decisions on containment and recovery, and have the designated owner assess notification duties, timelines and approved channels for each applicable authority.
Evidence examples
Incident timeline, severity rationale, evidence register and chain of custody, containment decisions, notification record where applicable, recovery validation, and a post-incident review with action owners.
Operational measures
Time from incident declaration to confirmed containment by severity, and the proportion of corrective actions closed by their due date.

Target operating model

Use the SOC-CMM paper as an operating model reference. The applicability and wording of Saudi regulatory requirements come from NCA and SAMA.

Defining and operationalising a SOC Target Operating Model

The paper describes how to set goals and a target date, assess current capability, agree on a target state, and turn the gaps into a continuous improvement backlog. Here is how to apply that sequence across the defense function.

  1. Assess the current stateReview SOC telemetry coverage, IR readiness and the quality of CTI handoffs. Record the evidence behind each gap.
  2. Set the target stateDefine the services, owners and success measures you want by the target date. Have the responsible leadership approve the target and its resources.
  3. Build the roadmapPrioritize gaps by risk and dependency. Give each item an owner and a completion check, then reassess progress against the baseline.

Official references

Use the scope and version published by each authority to identify the requirements relevant to your organization.

National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)

الهيئة الوطنية للأمن السيبراني

NCA publishes national cybersecurity controls and implementation guidance. Start with the scope in each control document.

Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC 2:2024)

Baseline controls for entities within the published scope, covering governance, defense, resilience and third-party cybersecurity.

Cloud Cybersecurity Controls (CCC 2:2024)

Cloud-specific controls extending ECC. Review the scope and requirements for the provider or tenant role that applies.

NCA implementation guides

Practical guidance for applying the published controls.

Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)

البنك المركزي السعودي

SAMA publishes cybersecurity requirements for regulated financial institutions. Use the Rulebook to identify requirements relevant to your institution.

SAMA Cyber Security Framework

Domains include leadership and governance, risk management and compliance, operations and technology, and third-party cybersecurity.

Framework applicability

Official guidance on entities and requirements within the framework’s scope.

Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles

Guidance on the intelligence lifecycle, from defining requirements to producing and sharing intelligence.

Start with applicability

Check the entity type and services in scope, then record the official version and control references. Assign an owner and document the evidence that demonstrates each applicable control. Where multiple frameworks apply, keep each requirement traceable to its official source.
